## 中文

论文用的全部插图（PDF）。**不要手动改这些 PDF**——它们由脚本生成，改了下次重跑会被覆盖。
一次重跑全部：`python tools/generate_figures.py`（跑子集：`python tools/generate_figures.py frozen bler`）。

## English

All figures used by the paper (PDF). **Do not hand-edit the PDFs** — they are generated and will be
overwritten. Regenerate everything with `python tools/generate_figures.py`, or a subset with
`python tools/generate_figures.py frozen bler`.

Six of the ten figures in the main paper come from **one** generator reading **one** frozen source
(P5-7 D). That generator also writes `../../results/provenance/PROVENANCE_figures.json`, the input to
`tools/check_figure_consistency.py`, which is what makes "the number in the figure is the number in
the caption" checkable rather than eyeballed.
